Elvin (Mingpu) Hu is a designer and researcher working on intent-aware and intelligent interfaces. He is a master's student at the Keio University Graduate School of Media Design, in the Embodied Media Lab.

Previously Senior Designer and Design Lead at Google, where he led design for Chrome's first Gemini-powered features, Chrome on Android and novel form factors, and the first refresh of the Chrome brand in eight years.
